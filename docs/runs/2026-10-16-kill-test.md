## Reading

- **The thesis holds on what the checker can see.** Arm C is the only arm in which the owner's conditions reach the reader (conditions 0.80 against 0.00) and in which the two forbidden readings are stopped or avoided every time (refusal 1.00 against 0.83): the Wallonia filter is refused in all five runs, and the sum over werkjaren is never asked for, because the model read the sentence in `describe` and chose a series. Arms A and B sum the stock over years and answer for Wallonia with a number, five times out of five each.
- **Reading the prose alone (B) buys little over nothing (A).** B's numbers are better than A's on the Leuven and Gent comparison (the ratio is used in three runs out of five against none) and nothing else moves.
- **Sameness is low in every arm.** Identical claims across five runs in a third of the questions in C and half in A; identical surviving numbers in one question out of twelve in B and C. The model varies the queries it runs (a series here, two single werkjaren there) even when the finding ends up the same. The checker does not make the reading deterministic; it makes the forbidden readings impossible.
- **Where C scores below expectation, the expectation is stricter than the behaviour.** On q03 and q07 the model in C sets the period and the type explicitly after reading the conditions, so the engine applies no default and the default's verdict does not appear, while the numbers are right in every run. q11 asks for an age the data does not hold; in arm C the model sees that in `describe` and never queries it, so no gap is recorded and *boundary* fails, while in arm A the model queries `leeftijd_leiding`, gets the error, and the gap is recorded. A gap is only recorded when the model asks.
- **Two runs in C ended without `finalize`**: the model wrote the answer as text after six calls. Scored as not answered.
- **q10 fails on numbers in every arm because of a tracing bug the run found**: the provincie value "Brussel (19 gemeenten)" put a "19" in the statements that was not a numeric cell, so the statement with the right numbers was dropped at finalize time. Fixed after the run (a number inside a dimension value is a label); the stored findings keep the drop.
